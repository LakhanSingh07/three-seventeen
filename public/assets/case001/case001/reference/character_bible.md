## Sarah Mehta

- **Age/background:** 22; fictional young Indian woman.
- **Face:** Soft oval face, warm medium-brown skin, almond-shaped dark-brown eyes, full natural brows, straight nose, subtle beauty mark below her right eye, gentle closed-mouth smile.
- **Hair:** Shoulder-length near-black hair, soft natural layers, side part, slight bend rather than curls.
- **Build:** Slim, everyday build.
- **Canonical styling:** Deep teal knit top, tiny silver studs, delicate crescent pendant, minimal makeup.
- **Visual personality:** Warm, observant, intelligent and quietly self-possessed; approachable without looking staged.
- **Identity locks:** Eye shape, right-eye beauty mark, jawline, shoulder-length side-parted hair and crescent pendant. Do not turn her into a glamorous model.
